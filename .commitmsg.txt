fix(ui): restore edge-to-edge; the system bars showed a grey band

Reported as "immersion is gone, top and bottom". It was.

MainActivity padded android.R.id.content by the system bar insets. Nothing
Compose draws covers that padding, so what showed through was the window
background - Theme.Material's #303030. Measured on the device: rows 0-117 and
2356-2399 were exactly #303030, while the app's own gradient is #171A22 at the
top fading to #0E1016 at the bottom. Hence two grey bands.

Inset handling moves to Compose, which is where the content that has to avoid the
bars actually lives:

  - the gradient fills the whole window, including behind both bars
  - the pages take WindowInsets.statusBars as top padding
  - LiquidGlassNavBar already resolves navigationBars on its own, and was in fact
    being double-counted before (root padding plus its own)

The backdrop recorder stays full-screen and only the inner Box is inset;
shrinking the recorded layer would desync the coordinates the glass bar samples
with, and the blur would drift.

Window background now matches the gradient's top colour, in themes.xml (what the
system paints before the app's first frame) and at runtime. That also removes the
grey flash on cold start, which had the same #303030 root cause as the bands.
